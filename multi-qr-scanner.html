Privacy Policy for Multi QR Scanner & Generator

Application: Multi QR Scanner & Generator • Developer: NEURON TS LABS • Last Updated: August 31, 2026

1. Privacy Architecture & Executive Summary

Welcome to Multi QR Scanner & Generator, developed by NEURON TS LABS. This application is engineered with an uncompromising offline-first, privacy-by-design architecture. Privacy is not a secondary setting; it is structurally embedded into every scanning, creation, batch processing, and export workflow.

Fundamental Privacy Directives:

  • 100% Offline: The core scanning and code generation engines require zero internet access.
  • No Network Permissions: The application does not declare the Android INTERNET permission for its core engine.
  • No Account Infrastructure: No sign-ups, accounts, logins, or remote user tracking.
  • Zero Remote Storage: Your scanned barcodes, generated designs, and scan histories reside solely on your physical device.
  • Zero Third-Party Tracking: No analytics beacons, tracking identifiers, or ad profiling SDKs.

2. Information Processed On-Device

Because Multi QR Scanner & Generator functions exclusively offline, all data processing takes place strictly inside your physical device's local memory environment:

A. Camera Live Viewfinder Data

When the camera scanner is active, the application accesses the camera feed ephemerally to detect 1D and 2D barcode patterns in real time. Zero frames, photographs, or video feeds are ever recorded, cached, or transmitted. The stream is processed in-memory and immediately released.

B. On-Device Content & History Generation

Any QR codes or barcodes you generate, customize, scan, favorite, or export are saved strictly inside an on-device local Room Database and encrypted application preferences via Android DataStore. This index is sandboxed and never synchronized with remote servers.

C. Local Gallery Image Decoding

If you choose to scan a barcode from an existing device photo or screenshot, the image URI is consumed locally using on-device machine learning detection models. The source image is never duplicated, modified, or uploaded off-device.

5. Storage Architecture & Security Isolation

Data stored by Multi QR Scanner & Generator is protected through multiple platform-level safeguards:

  • Sandboxed Database: QR scan logs and favorites are stored in private internal application storage that cannot be queried by other installed third-party apps.
  • Backup Exclusion: Local databases are excluded from automatic cloud extraction rules to ensure private codes are not uploaded during generic Android backups.
  • Zero Network Attack Surface: Because the application does not utilize network execution threads, remote data exfiltration vectors are eliminated.

6. User Data Rights & Erasure Controls

You maintain complete and autonomous sovereignty over your data lifecycle:

  • Individual Record Deletion: Select and wipe specific scans or created items directly from the History screen.
  • Global Cache Flush: Clear your entire history, favorite tags, and custom design presets in a single tap via in-app settings.
  • Complete OS Erasure: Performing an uninstallation or selecting Clear Storage / Data in Android Settings permanently wipes all databases and preferences from the physical flash memory.

7. Children's Privacy & Regulatory Compliance

Multi QR Scanner & Generator is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. Given our total absence of data harvesting, tracking cookies, user profiling, and cloud databases, the application inherently satisfies global privacy frameworks, including COPPA, GDPR, and Indian digital data protection standards.
